MindCodeNov 2026NOVA hackathon · ScottyLabs, CMUTeam lead

Every assumption an AI makes, as a map you can edit before it builds.

When AI builds an app, it silently decides dozens of things you never asked for. MindCode turns your idea into a full plan: pages, features, APIs, design, audience and cost, colored by what you said vs. what Claude assumed vs. what's still open. Then it builds the app and traces every element back to the bubble that caused it.

  • Designed the prompt-engineering framework (Design / Audience / Functionality / Cost) that structures every plan.
  • Led 4 people and a team of AI coding agents (Claude Code, Google Antigravity, Jules) through a shared task board, cross-review and a 15-deploy budget.
  • Right-click any part of the generated site to change it (~7 s, via patch edits); small plan changes rebuild in ~20 s.

Ben's Notes2026TypeScript · PWA · 72 commitsSolo

A notebook that reviews your notes as you write them.

Ben's Notes (also called Pipit Notes) is an iPad notebook wired to machine-learning models. While you write with Apple Pencil, it reads your handwriting, checks it, and turns it into study material built on learning-science methods that have been tested in research. Notes stay on your device in IndexedDB, with no account and no server.

  • Live review as you write: handwriting is recognized into math and text, each line of working is checked (SymPy running in the browser through Pyodide verifies the algebra), and mistakes are highlighted on the page with why they're wrong and the fix.
  • Exams that look like your real ones: timed practice papers generated from your notes and course PDFs, with structured multi-part questions, graphs, tables and diagrams cut from your own materials, marked against a marking scheme.
  • Proven study methods: FSRS-5 spaced repetition (the scheduler Anki uses), retrieval practice, interleaved short lessons, and concept maps you recall from memory and that are colored by how well you know each idea.

Pipit Finance2026Next.js · Prisma · PostgresSolo · private

A full personal-finance platform, connected to real bank accounts.

A budgeting and net-worth app in the spirit of Monarch or Copilot, built end to end: about 440 files, 38 database models and 88 commits. Bank accounts sync through Plaid and subscriptions bill through Stripe.

  • Bank sync: Plaid Link and incremental transaction sync, with signature-verified webhooks (JWT). Access tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Security: NextAuth sessions, TOTP two-factor auth with QR enrolment, rate limiting, and Zod validation on server input.
  • Money logic: a rules engine and auto-categorizer, recurring-charge detection, budgets, shared household goals, investment history, cash-balance forecasts and net-worth projections.
  • Reports: Sankey cash-flow diagrams (d3-sankey), spending reports, salary benchmarks, CSV import and export, and scheduled snapshot and alert jobs with email through Resend.
